Unusuale

A daily guessing game based on Wikipedia's underrated Unusual Articles Page.

Unusuale is a short daily game in that consists of several rounds. Each round features a question that is centered around a specific article from the Unusual Articles Page on Wikipedia. Correct answers give you points, and try to get as many points as you can. Questions also come in multiple forms, which you can read about in the section below.

Please Note
At the end of the day, while scoring high is the main objective of the game, the actual goal of this site is to pique interest into some of the strangest articles Wikipedia has to offer. If you find yourself reading one of them after playing, then the game has fulfilled its purpose.

Question Types

The current list of question types is as follows:

  • Factles ask you to answer a fact about the article, with four possible options.
  • Fakles ask you to choose the real unusual article out of four possible options.
  • Identifiables give you a snippet from an article that you must identiy, with four possible options.
  • Textles ask you to answer an open-ended question about the article.
  • Numerles ask you to guess a number related to the article. Partial credit based on the proximity to the answer.
  • Continentles ask you to determine the continent where an unusual place is located.
